I work on AI for DJing: which song comes next, where it comes in, and how to get from one track to the other without anyone on the dance floor noticing the seam.

I'm a PhD student in the Data Science Degree Program at National Taiwan University and Academia Sinica, working with Li Su and Yi-Hsuan Yang.

Most of what I build is for pop, where the vocals make everything harder. Two EDM tracks can overlap for a minute; two pop songs usually can't overlap for more than a few bars before the singers start singing over each other. So a lot of my work is about timing: finding the few places in a song where a mix can actually happen, and checking afterwards that it did. I've also spent time on drum transcription and source separation with latent diffusion.

Before the PhD I worked on electronic dance music classification at Academia Sinica, and as a machine learning engineer at CloudMile, on customer lifetime value models and LLM projects.
